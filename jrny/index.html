JRNY

A guided storytelling app that helps people capture memories, organize them into chapters and shape them into stories they keep or share. It starts from a single memory, theme or prompt, so nobody faces a blank page.

A place to turn life into story.

Chapter 1 · The moveChapter 2 · First jobChapter 3 · Turning points

What it does

  • Starts from a memory, a theme or a prompt, so the first step is never a blank page.
  • Stories grow chapter by chapter through small additions, organized around people, places, lessons and turning points.
  • Guided prompts help a person find the next sentence without taking over their voice.
  • Each story is shared on the owner's terms: kept private, or shared with family, a community or a wider audience.
