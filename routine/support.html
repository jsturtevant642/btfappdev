Account and billing

  • To delete your ROUTINE account, open the app and go to Profile > Delete Account.
  • Apple subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID subscription settings. Deleting your ROUTINE account does not cancel an active subscription.
  • For purchase or restore issues, use Restore Purchases in the app paywall or Profile screen.
