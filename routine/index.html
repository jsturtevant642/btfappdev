How it works

A session for this day, not the week you meant to have.

ROUTINE is built for busy adults whose weeks do not match rigid plans. Tell it your life. It builds the work. Then it keeps the line toward your goal, whether you showed up or life got in the way.

01

Tell ROUTINE what you are working toward.

Goals, equipment, time, and constraints become the starting point. Getting ready for a hike counts.

02

Get a session that fits today.

Hotel gym, twenty minutes, or a full lift. The answer is the next useful session, not a lecture.

03

Hold progress on misses. Level up on hits.

Missed sessions are information, not failure. When you earn it, ROUTINE suggests how to progress. Review and accept the change when you are ready.
