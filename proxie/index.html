Proxie

An AI representative that answers a recruiter's questions about a candidate, in the third person and only from material the candidate has approved. It is built on a structured, sourced model of the person's career, shared through tiered access codes.

What it does

  • An AI representative a professional shares by link. It answers a recruiter's or hiring manager's questions about them in the third person, from material they have approved.
  • Its knowledge is a structured model of the person: roles, projects, capabilities and stories, each linked to the others and to the sources behind them.
  • That structure lets it answer everything about one role, or trace a single capability across a whole career in order, which a plain document search cannot do reliably.
  • Access is tiered. Anyone with the link gets a substantive public overview; project-level and behavioral detail opens with a personal access code; private material never surfaces.
  • Each code belongs to one recipient, so the candidate sees who engaged, what they asked and how deeply they explored.
  • Every claim traces back to a source, and the person's own reflections appear as direct quotes rather than words attributed to them.
  • A job description attached to a code lets it frame the candidate's experience against what that role needs.
  • The first piece is live: Joshua Sturtevant's resume site, with per-recipient access codes, runs on the same profile files Proxie will answer from.
