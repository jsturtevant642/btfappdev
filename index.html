Audio storytelling Working prototype

JRNY

An AI audio storytelling app for drives, walks and other in-between moments. A listener picks a mood, a kind of story and how much time they have, and JRNY delivers a narrated story written by AI and voiced with synthetic narration.

  • Builds a story around the moment: pick a mood, a genre and the kind of energy you want, from calm to adventure.
  • Fits the time you have, with stories sized for a short walk, a commute or a long drive (5, 15 or 30 minutes).
  • AI writes each story and a synthetic narrator voices it, with read-along text that scrolls as you listen.

Career representation In developmentFirst site live

Proxie

Proxie builds one structured, sourced model of everything a professional has done. Every surface reads from it: a portfolio site, a skill galaxy, an AI representative and a brief that fits their record to a specific role.

1
Person Model behind every surface
3
access tiers: public, deep, private
2
ways to retrieve: by role, by skill
  • Intake works like coaching. Before anyone opens the link, Proxie lists the questions a hiring manager is likely to ask, and the person answers them first.
  • The representative speaks about the person in the third person and never invents experience. Their own reflections appear only as marked quotes.
  • Attach a job description to an access code and it maps each requirement to evidence from the record, and says plainly where the gaps are.
  • Each recipient gets their own code, so the person can see who opened the site and what they read.
