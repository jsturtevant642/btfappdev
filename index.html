Audio storytelling Working prototype

JRNY

An AI audio storytelling app for drives, walks and other in-between moments. A listener picks a mood, a kind of story and how much time they have, and JRNY delivers a narrated story written by AI and voiced with synthetic narration.

  • Builds a story around the moment: pick a mood, a genre and the kind of energy you want, from calm to adventure.
  • Fits the time you have, with stories sized for a short walk, a commute or a long drive (5, 15 or 30 minutes).
  • AI writes each story and a synthetic narrator voices it, with read-along text that scrolls as you listen.
