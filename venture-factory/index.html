Venture Factory

An AI marketing engine built to run several ventures from one system. Each venture plugs in a brand pack and a manifest of its data sources. One shared engine plans content, drafts it in the brand's voice, publishes it across social channels and reports on what's working, with built-in checks that strip out generic AI phrasing. Nothing publishes without passing a human review gate.

What it does

  • Runs marketing for several ventures from one shared engine. Each venture plugs in a brand pack built from a brand interview, plus a manifest of its data sources.
  • Every Monday an intelligence hub pulls Search Console, GA4, Mixpanel, payments and app data into a pulse: headline stats to act on, then more detailed, actionable insights.
  • A trend scout turns the week's news into evaluated content angles, and a planner weighs them against the content calendar and the pulse to pick what to publish.
  • The content engine drafts answer-first blog posts structured for search and AI citation, then cascades each one into LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook and email versions, carousel slides included.
  • Social drafting covers posts, threads, carousels and polls in each brand's voice, and engine-level checks strip out generic AI phrasing before a draft reaches review.
  • Daily engagement scouting finds real conversations worth joining, from influencer posts to people asking for help on Reddit and Quora, drafts replies in the brand's voice and recommends accounts to follow.
  • Monthly scans track competitors, search openings and how often AI assistants mention the brand across a fixed battery of prompts.
  • Nothing publishes until a person approves it. A publishing tool then posts to each channel and logs every post.
  • Runs today for ROUTINE and the band Start Forward, with a version for Remix Career.
