What it does

  • Turns executive coach Steven Miyao's methodology into AI-amplified career strategy and job application tools that mid-career professionals use to understand themselves and take action on their next chapter.
  • A 15-question reflective assessment feeds eight AI modules, and each module builds on what the earlier ones produced.
  • AI extracts six behavioral dimensions from a person's own words. Deterministic scoring matches them to 16 career archetypes, so results stay consistent.
  • Every report names three recommended moves, each with a first step and a read on how AI is changing that path.
  • Career remixes suggest unconventional uses for transferable skills a person may never have named.
  • Creator tools rewrite a resume, cover letter or LinkedIn profile for a specific job posting, built on the strengths the report surfaced. A free AI Impact Check covers 100 roles.
  • Launched publicly in late April 2026 and had its first paying customer within three weeks.
